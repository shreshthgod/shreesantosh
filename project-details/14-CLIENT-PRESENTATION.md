# 14 — Client Presentation (non-technical)

## What was improved (and why it matters to the business)

**1. Buyers' questions now answer themselves.**
A new FAQ section covers grades, pack sizes, on-demand retail packs, custom packing, export support, and how to enquire. Fewer confused visitors, more completed enquiries — and Google can show these answers directly in search results.

**2. Google-ready foundations.**
The site now tells search engines what to index (sitemap, robots), shows rich previews when links are shared (image cards for WhatsApp/X/Facebook), and carries machine-readable business + FAQ data. Nothing about the design changed; discoverability did.

**3. No more dead ends.**
 mistyped URLs and rare errors now land on a branded page that guides visitors back to grades or the enquiry form — instead of a technical error screen.

**4. Smoother enquiry form.**
"Not sure" is now an option for quantity as well as grade, so uncertain buyers keep going instead of abandoning. All product images now describe themselves to screen readers.

**5. Launch checklist honesty.**
Contact details (phone/WhatsApp/email/address) and the website address are clearly flagged as the two things you must provide before launch — everything else is ready.

## Visual / experience improvements

- New FAQ matches the existing premium language (serif headlines, hairline dividers, gold accents) — it looks like it was always there.
- Form, buttons, and animations untouched where they already excelled; we improved, not restyled.

## Business value

- Higher enquiry completion (fewer stalls, clearer packs, FAQ objection-handling).
- Better first impression in shared links and search.
- A site you can show a distributor or export buyer today with confidence — pending only your real contact details.

## Future roadmap (optional, quoted separately)

Server-saved enquiries with email alerts, visitor analytics, Hindi/Bengali content, verified testimonials and lab reports. Each is scoped in the handoff so you can prioritise.
